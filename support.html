❓ FAQ

Q: Does the game require internet?

A: No. Trivia Maze works fully offline. All questions are included in the app.

Q: How do I unlock the full game?

A: Open the app and select Unlock Full Game (Trivia Maze+ upgrade). This is a one-time purchase that unlocks all levels beyond Level 3.

Q: I found a bug.

A: Please email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the issue occurred.

Q: How do I restore my purchase?

A: Use the Restore Purchase option inside the app’s paywall screen. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID.

Q: Do you support more topics and languages?

A: Yes — we plan to expand topics and add more languages over time. Feedback is always welcome.
