Genie question gates

When a path is locked, the genie appears to guide you forward with a fast 4-answer question. You have 3 chances to answer correctly and open the way. If all attempts are used, the maze resets and you begin again.

Clean, readable maze play

Simple controls and clear visuals let you focus on navigating the maze — not menus. Start the adventure and immerse yourself in trivia as you explore.

Party Mode (2–6 players)

Play together on one device. Players take turns answering questions and competing for the highest score — perfect for family nights and quick challenges with friends. Mirror the game to a big screen using AirPlay or Screen Mirroring so everyone can follow along. Optional read-aloud can speak the questions and answer choices, making Party Mode even easier to enjoy in a group.

Topics & difficulty

Start with History, Geography, Math and General Knowledge. Choose your difficulty — Beginner, Skilled, or Expert — and progress through 25 levels per topic. Works offline with all 6,000 questions included in the game. Keep a daily streak (one game per day) to unlock new “magic” color themes: 3 days brings a new look, and 1–2 weeks unlocks even more.

Short sessions, real progression

Quick levels designed for short play sessions that feel rewarding. Each level opens new paths, blending fun with learning into progress that lasts. This isn’t just a game — it’s an advantage. Knowledge is power.
